How it works

Three steps. Then it counts on its own.

  1. Write down what happened

    A short title is enough: "Replied 'k'." Add a note, a category and a heat level if you like.

  2. Say when it started

    Now, last week, or years ago. Backdate it as far as you need. The clock picks up from that moment.

  3. Watch it count

    Every grudge shows a live timer. Time is worked out from the date itself, so it stays exact even if the app is closed for months.

Features

Everything a grudge needs. Nothing it doesn't.

Grudge Clock is a record-keeping tool with a dry sense of humour. It doesn't judge, coach or remind you to let go.

A live clock on every grudge

Your list shows each grudge with its running time: "6 years, 2 months, 17 days". The longest one is marked as the oldest. Sort by oldest, newest, hottest or A–Z, filter by category, and search titles, notes and subtopics.

Each grudge has a heat level:

  • MILD
  • SIMMERING
  • BURNING
  • VOLCANIC

Every second, accounted for

Open a grudge to see its clock at full size, the exact moment it started, and what's next on the calendar.

  • Milestones at one week, one month, three and six months, one year, two years and five years, with optional notifications.
  • On this day: a reminder on a grudge's anniversary.
  • Notes and subtopics for grievances with several parts.
  • Photo evidence: attach the screenshot. A private copy stays in the app.

Stats that don't flatter you

The numbers, laid out plainly: grudges logged, currently active, resolved, your longest active and longest resolved grudge, the average time to resolution, and your favourite heat level and category.

Plus the combined total of time spent holding grudges, and your current streak: how many consecutive days you've held at least one. "2,270 days without letting go" is a real thing the app will tell you.

{{img phone-stats "Grudge Clock stats: combined grudge time, grudges logged, active and resolved, and the current streak"}}

Let go, if you want. The record stays.

Resolve a grudge when you're ready and add a closure note. It moves to the resolved archive with its final time frozen. Changed your mind? Reactivate it, and its earlier resolutions stay on record.

Delete is there too, with undo for the moments you were hasty.

Privacy

Your grudges stay on your phone

There's no account and no cloud. Nobody, including us, can read what you write.

  1. Offline by design

    Everything you log is stored on your device. The app works without a connection.

  2. Backups you control

    Create a backup file with your records and photos, or export them as text. You decide where it goes.

  3. Discreet when you want

    Notifications are opt-in and can hide titles from your lock screen. Receipts can hide titles, dates and categories before you share.

The app is free and shows ads through Google AdMob, which asks for consent where the law requires it. Your titles, notes and photos are never passed to it. Details are in the privacy policy.
