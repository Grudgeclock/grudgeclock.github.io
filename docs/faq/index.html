Frequently asked questions

The app

What is Grudge Clock?

A grudge tracker for Android. You log a grudge, meaning what happened and when it started, and the app shows a live count of exactly how long you've held it, from minutes to years. It adds heat levels, categories, notes, photo evidence, milestones, stats and a shareable receipt. It deliberately gives no therapy advice and doesn't tell you to forgive anyone.

Is Grudge Clock free?

Yes. Every feature is free. There are no subscriptions and no in-app purchases. The app shows ads to pay for itself.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet. Grudge Clock is available on Android through Google Play.

Which languages does it support?

English, Brazilian Portuguese and Indonesian. The app follows your phone's language setting.

Can I add a grudge that started years ago?

Yes. Set any start date within the last 50 years. The clock counts from that moment, so a grudge from 2019 shows its full age straight away.

Does the clock keep running when the app is closed?

Yes. The time is calculated from the start date each time you look, not from a timer running in the background. It's exact even if you don't open the app for months.

What happens when I resolve a grudge?

It moves to the resolved archive with its final time frozen, and you can add a closure note. You can reactivate it later; its earlier resolutions stay in its history.

Privacy and data

Does my data leave my phone?

No. Everything you log, including titles, notes and photos, is stored on your device only. There is no account, no sign-up and no cloud sync, and we can't see or recover what you write. The ad SDK can collect limited device data such as the advertising ID, under Google's policies; it never receives your records. See the privacy policy.

How do I back up my grudges?

In Settings, choose Create backup. You get one file with your records and photos that you can save wherever you like, and restore later with Restore backup. You can also export your records as plain text. Backup files aren't encrypted, so keep them somewhere private.

If I attach a photo, is the original moved or deleted?

No. The app keeps its own private copy. Deleting the photo in the app removes that copy and leaves the original in your photo library alone.

Can I hide grudge titles in notifications?

Yes, and they're hidden by default. Notifications are off until you turn them on, and titles stay off your lock screen unless you choose to show them.

The receipt

What is the grudge receipt?

A till-receipt image of your grudges: each one itemised with its running time, totals at the bottom, your current streak and a verdict. You can print one for all your grudges or for a single one, and share it as an image. More about the receipt.

Can I share a receipt without revealing details?

Yes. Choose which records appear, give them public titles or leave them as "Undisclosed grievance", and hide titles, dates, categories or closure notes. The receipt is made on your phone; nothing is uploaded.

Ads

Why are there ads?

They keep the app free. There's at most one full-screen ad per session, shown after you save a new grudge once you have a few, and a small banner on the main list. Where the law requires it, you're asked for consent first, and you can change your choice in Settings under Advertising choices.

Can I pay to remove ads?

Not at the moment. Everything is free and supported by ads only.

Everything else

Is Grudge Clock a mental health app?

No. It's a record-keeping app with a dry sense of humour. It isn't medical, psychological or legal advice. If something is weighing on you, talk to someone you trust or a professional.
