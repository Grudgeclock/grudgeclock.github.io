The receipt

Your grudges, printed on a receipt

Grudge Clock turns what you're holding into a till receipt: every grievance itemised with its running time, totals at the bottom, and a verdict nobody asked for. Share it as an image, wherever you like.

Get it on Google Play
Two kinds

A receipt of grievances, or just the one

From your stats, print the receipt of grievances: up to six records with their durations, how many are active and resolved, your current streak, and the combined time.

From any single grudge, print one (1) grievance: its title, how long it has been running, its heat level, category and start date. Resolved grudges print as case closed, with their final time.

Both end the same way: NO REFUNDS · NO FORGIVENESS.

The verdict

Every receipt gets a verdict

At the bottom of each receipt, Grudge Clock sums up the total time in one line. The line changes as the total grows.

Total time on the receiptVerdict
Nothing yet"Nothing on record. Yet."
Under a week"Just getting started."
Under a month"Warming up."
Under six months"Established."
Under a year"This is a lifestyle now."
Under three years"A career, at this point."
Three years or more"Historic. Genuinely historic."
Before you share

Share the number, not the details

A receipt shows exactly what its preview shows, and you choose what that is.

  1. Pick the records

    Choose up to six grudges for the receipt. The totals count only the ones you picked.

  2. Give them public titles

    Rename an item for the receipt only, or leave it as "Undisclosed grievance". The real title stays in the app.

  3. Hide what you like

    Hide titles, dates, categories or closure notes. The receipt is made on your phone; nothing is uploaded.

Why a receipt

Because "receipts" means proof

In slang, keeping receipts means saving evidence of what someone said or did. Grudge Clock keeps the date, the details and, if you attach one, the screenshot. The receipt is the printout.
